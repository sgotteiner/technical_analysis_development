S/R lines at "now"

Lines use only candles up to "now" (bright candles). Faded candles are the future, shown so you can judge whether the lines held. Solid = where the line acted as S/R (first to last touch), dashed = extended through now and 30 days ahead. Thick = higher level, thin = lower level.

Definition: the line (any slope) running through the most significant peaks and valleys of the period - it may split the graph, touching both, and small overshoots do not move it. Peaks / valleys are by magnitude; a touch counts when price moved the level's magnitude away from the line on both sides. Each level's lower + upper line form a pipe that must not widen, both lines act at the same time, and its width lies between the median and the largest swing move (checked where it starts and at now).
